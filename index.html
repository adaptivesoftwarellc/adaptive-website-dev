The situation we usually walk into

Too many systems, and none of them connected.

Most organizations add software one need at a time: a scheduler, a billing portal, a few spreadsheets, and an older records system that still holds important history.

Each tool works on its own. Together, they mean several logins to answer one question, the same information entered more than once, and reports that are only as accurate as the last manual update.

  • Double entry The same information is entered into several systems.
  • Conflicting reports Two reports disagree, and no one is sure which is correct.
  • Hard-to-reach records Older records are kept but can't easily be searched.
  • Overlapping costs Several subscriptions, each charging for the same users.

Is this you?

Sound familiar?

  • More than three systems that don't share information.
  • The same information gets entered twice because nothing syncs.
  • Answering a simple question means checking several systems.
  • Your oldest records are kept, but no one can easily search them.
  • A spreadsheet holds the only copy of something you can't lose.
  • Your vendor will never add the report you need.

If two or more apply, there's likely a better way to work. We'll talk it through with you, and we'll be honest if building something isn't the right answer.

Solutions

Two ways we help, usually in this order.

  • Consolidation

    One application instead of many

    We learn how work actually moves through your team, then build a single application that supports it from start to finish. Fewer logins, one source of truth, and no re-entering information between systems.

  • Custom Builds

    Software for what's missing

    When off-the-shelf software almost fits, we build the rest: integrations between systems, internal tools, and the reports your current vendor doesn't offer.

Our Process

Four steps. You see working software in the first month.

  1. 01

    Map

    We sit with the people doing the work and document every system, handoff, and workaround. You get the map whether or not you hire us for the rest.

  2. 02

    Build

    We build in small pieces you can use and give feedback on along the way, so there are no surprises at launch.

  3. 03

    Migrate

    We move your existing data, verify it against the source, and make sure everything is accounted for before anything is switched off.

  4. 04

    Support

    Software you depend on needs someone to call. We stay on for maintenance, changes, and the next thing you need.
